Who We Are

Crafting Trips That Feel Personal, Safe & Truly Magical

Spree Holidays (@snowspreeholidays) was born out of a deep reverence for the Kashmir Valley. We saw travelers struggling with fragmented bookings, inconsistent drivers, and impersonal customer service.

We set out to change that by building an end-to-end, full-service travel company based right here in Srinagar. When you book with Spree Holidays, you are not dealing with middlemen or generic national portals. You are speaking directly with local specialists who know every mountain pass, every weather change, and every traditional houseboat owner on Dal and Nigeen Lakes.

From the moment our representative receives you at Jammu Railway Station or Srinagar International Airport, to your final farewell with a box of fresh Kashmiri walnuts and saffron, our promise is absolute reliability and genuine Kashmiri warmth (*Mehmaan-nawazi*).
