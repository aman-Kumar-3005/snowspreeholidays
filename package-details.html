Day-By-Day Plan

Day 1

Arrival in Srinagar • Sunset Shikara & Houseboat Check-in

▼

Arrive at Srinagar International Airport (or private pickup from Jammu Railway Station). Our chauffeur warmly receives you and drives you along the scenic boulevard to Dal Lake.

Check into your hand-carved heritage wooden houseboat. In the late afternoon, enjoy a complimentary 1-hour relaxing Shikara ride on Dal Lake, visiting the floating gardens, Nehru Park, and Char Chinar island. Savor warm traditional Kashmiri Kahwa on deck as the sun sets behind the Zabarwan mountains.

Houseboat Check-in Shikara Ride Welcome Kahwa & Dinner
Day 2

Srinagar to Gulmarg • Gondola Cable Car & Snow Activities

▼

After breakfast on the houseboat, embark on a picturesque drive to Gulmarg ("Meadow of Flowers", approx. 52 km / 1.5 hours) passing through Tangmarg's pine woods.

Board the famous Gulmarg Gondola (world's second-highest operating cable car). Ascend to Phase 1 (Kongdoori) and Phase 2 (Apharwat Peak at 13,780 ft) for breathtaking panoramic vistas of snow peaks. Enjoy skiing, snowmobiling, or sledge rides. Return to Srinagar hotel in the evening for dinner.

Gulmarg Gondola Apharwat Peak Snow Biking & Skiing
Day 3

Srinagar to Pahalgam • Saffron Fields & Betaab Valley

▼

Depart after breakfast toward Pahalgam ("Valley of Shepherds", approx. 95 km / 2.5 hours). En route, pause at the historic town of Pampore to see lush saffron fields and visit dry fruit farms.

Drive along the glistening Lidder River. Explore Betaab Valley (named after the Bollywood movie), the lush meadows of Aru Valley, and Chandanwari (gateway to the sacred Amarnath yatra). Check into your riverside hotel in Pahalgam for an evening of peaceful mountain ambiance.

Pampore Saffron Betaab Valley Lidder River Riverside Hotel
Day 4

Pahalgam to Anantnag Apple Orchards • Return to Srinagar

▼

Enjoy breakfast surrounded by pine-covered hills. On your return journey toward Srinagar, take a scenic detour through Anantnag & Apple Valley.

Walk through genuine Kashmiri apple orchards, taste crisp fresh apples straight from the trees (seasonal in autumn), and visit the monumental 8th-century ruins of the Martand Sun Temple. Reach Srinagar by late afternoon; visit the terraced Mughal Gardens (Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh, & Chashme Shahi) overlooking Dal Lake.

Apple Orchard Walk Martand Sun Temple Mughal Gardens
Day 5

Excursion to Sonmarg • Thajiwas Glacier & Sindh River

▼

Full-day day excursion to Sonmarg ("Meadow of Gold", approx. 80 km / 2 hours), situated along the Sindh River with towering glacier peaks on all sides.

Trek or hire a local pony to the magnificent Thajiwas Glacier, where snow remains year-round. Enjoy snowball fights, sledge slides, and roadside trout fish delicacies. Return to Srinagar hotel in the evening for dinner and packing.

Sonmarg Alpine Vistas Thajiwas Glacier Sindh River
Day 6

Old City Souvenirs & Airport Farewell

▼

After a leisurely breakfast, take some time for last-minute souvenir shopping in Srinagar for world-famous Pashmina shawls, walnut wood carvings, saffron, and dry fruits.

Our chauffeur drops you back at Srinagar International Airport (or Jammu Tawi Station) with wonderful memories of your Kashmir holiday with Spree Holidays.

Pashmina & Walnut Wood Airport Drop Trip Complete
