Welcome to Spree Holidays

Your Local Hosts in Paradise on Earth

Based right here in Srinagar, Spree Holidays was founded with one genuine mission: to make visiting Kashmir effortless, warm, and deeply unforgettable.

Whether you are arriving at Jammu railway station or landing at Srinagar airport, our private chauffeurs welcome you with Kashmiri warmth (*Mehmaan-nawazi*). We coordinate pristine hotel rooms, authentic heritage houseboats on Dal Lake, scenic gondola journeys in Gulmarg, and quiet walks through Anantnag's fragrant apple valleys.

From multi-generational family getaways to energetic Gen-Z group vacations and romantic honeymoon setups, we tailor every moment so you never have to worry about logistics.
