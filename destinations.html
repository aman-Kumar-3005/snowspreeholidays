Summer Capital & Heritage

Srinagar & Dal Lake Houseboats

Srinagar is the romantic heart of the Kashmir Valley, cradled along the banks of the Jhelum River and Dal Lake. Famed for its ornate, cedar-carved houseboats and vibrant Shikaras, Srinagar lets you experience a floating lifestyle unlike anywhere else in the world.

Wake up to birdsong and floating vegetable vendors, cruise along the water alleys to Char Chinar island, and explore the tiered Mughal Gardens—Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh, and Chashme Shahi—built by Mughal emperors with views stretching to the Zabarwan peaks.

Meadow of Flowers & Snow

Gulmarg & Apharwat Peak

Located at 8,690 feet, Gulmarg transforms from a carpet of vibrant wildflowers in summer into Asia's premier winter sports wonderland in winter. It is famous worldwide for the Gulmarg Gondola, which lifts travelers up to Kongdoori (Phase 1) and onto Apharwat Peak (Phase 2 at nearly 14,000 feet).

From snowball fights with family to powder skiing, snowboarding, and snowmobile safaris, Gulmarg is an absolute must on any Kashmir holiday. Enjoy warm tea at hillside cafes overlooking pine forests smothered in fresh white snow.

Valley of Shepherds

Pahalgam & Betaab Valley

Nestled along the rushing, crystal-clear Lidder River, Pahalgam is loved for its pine-scented mountain air, gentle pastoral beauty, and dramatic Bollywood movie backdrops.

Explore Betaab Valley with its rolling green lawns and surrounding peaks, ride ponies through dense pine forests to Baisaran ("Mini Switzerland"), and drive up to Aru Valley, the trailhead for pristine alpine trekking routes. Pahalgam is also famous for fresh river trout angling and white-water river rafting during summer.

Meadow of Gold

Sonmarg & Thajiwas Glacier

True to its name, Sonmarg ("Meadow of Gold") sits along the ancient Silk Route at 9,000 feet, guarded by jagged snow-capped peaks and the roaring Sindh River.

The headline attraction here is the Thajiwas Glacier, accessible on foot or pony back, where snow remains throughout mid-summer. It is also the gateway to Ladakh via the dramatic Zoji La Pass. Sonmarg is the perfect day excursion for travelers seeking raw, high-altitude alpine grandeur.

Orchards & Heritage

Anantnag & Apple Valley

South of Srinagar lies Anantnag, celebrated for its mineral water springs, fragrant pine forests, and vast orchards that earn it the title of Kashmir's Apple Valley.

During harvest season (August to November), the trees are heavy with crisp red Kashmiri apples. Guests can stroll directly through family-owned orchards, pick fresh fruit, taste genuine saffron at Pampore, and marvel at the majestic stone columns of the 8th-century Martand Sun Temple ruins.
